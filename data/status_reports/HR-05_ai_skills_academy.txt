1. WHAT'S GOING WELL
-------------------------------------------------
Enrolment is ahead of plan. 2,140 employees have started the "AI Foundations" e-learning path (target for year end was 2,000 - already exceeded). Completion rate of those who started is 68%.

Manager workshops ("Leading teams with AI") have run 12 times, 190 managers attended.

-------------------------------------------------
2. CONCERNS
-------------------------------------------------
a) Responsible AI module
We committed to a mandatory Responsible AI module in the Foundations path. The content was supposed to come from the AI Governance working group (GOV-03) on 1 October. We still don't have it. We can't make the path mandatory for all staff without it, and Legal won't let us write it ourselves. If we don't have content by early November, the mandatory roll-out slips to Q1 2027. Medium impact - it's mostly a compliance / reputational thing, but also affects the EU AI Act AI-literacy obligation that Legal keeps mentioning.

b) Role-specific training for copilot rollouts
We've booked training sessions in weeks 45-46 for ~350 customer service agents ahead of the Customer Service Copilot wave 2. We are now hearing informally that wave 2 might slip to December. If it does, we need to rebook ~30 sessions and trainers, and training too far ahead of go-live doesn't stick. Need a firm date from CRM-01 by 25 Oct.

Same story for the Sales Proposal Generator (SAL-08) - we planned sales team training for January but have no confirmed go-live date.

c) Licence costs for the learning platform add-on
The AI practice sandbox add-on (lets people practise prompting safely) costs €65k/year. Funded for 2026 only from the transformation budget. Not yet in the 2027 budget. Low-medium risk.
